# Backlog — everything open, in one place

The single registry of work we have said we will do and have not yet done. It merges the old
`deferred.md` (slice deferrals), the old design-proposal backlog, and the open tails of every
completed arc directory (deleted from `plans/` once shipped — their full slice histories and
design sketches live in git history).

**Discipline:** when an arc defers something non-gate, add a row here in the same commit. When a
slice or arc picks an item up, strike the row and point to the work that closed it. Each row names
its **source** and, where useful, a concrete **trigger** — the condition that should make us do it,
so we neither do it prematurely nor forget it. Items marked *(active)* are worth picking up on their
own; everything else is trigger-gated.

Nothing here is a correctness gap in shipped behavior unless explicitly marked **bug**.

| **Interruptible host IO — the one case `h.cancel()` still cannot reach.** The isolate-cancel slice made a cancel a real request that crosses the thread boundary: a worker polls the flag at the dispatch loop's frame transfers and taken loop back-edges (the safepoints the GC already had) and at every round of its own scheduler, so a *compute-bound* isolate now genuinely stops — measured 3.86s → 0.21s on a 40M-iteration loop cancelled at 200ms — and `join` reports `Ok(v)` for work that finished and `Err(Cancelled)` only for work that actually stopped. What it cannot do is preempt a worker blocked **inside a native call**: a `Process.read_line`, a socket read, a blocking syscall. That thread is not executing Noeta, reaches no safepoint, and the enclosing `concurrent` block's closing brace waits for it. **Waiting is the deliberate choice and should stay one** — abandoning the thread is what the old behavior did, and it was not merely untidy: the abandoned worker raced the parent's exit-time heap teardown and segfaulted in the allocator, reproducibly (`crates/noeta-cli/tests/cli/isolates.rs::run_real_isolate_cancel_is_joined_before_the_block_closes` is the regression guard).
